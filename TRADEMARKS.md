# Trademark Policy

## What the mark covers

"Snag" and the Snag logo identify the canonical project maintained by
Gaurav Albal (and any future Snag entity). The name and logo are used here as
trademarks to distinguish the canonical project and its distributions.

## What this policy allows

- **Accurate attribution.** You may refer to the project by name when
  describing it accurately: "Snag", "derived from Snag", "compatible with
  Snag", "a fork of Snag".
- **Fork naming.** Forks may accurately describe themselves as derived from or
  compatible with Snag.

## What requires permission

- Using "Snag" as the **product name** of a fork, derivative, or competing
  distribution without permission.
- Using the Snag logo in a way that implies endorsement by or affiliation with
  the canonical project.
- Registering or using "Snag" in a way that could create confusion about the
  source of the software.

Contact the maintainers for permission in these cases.

## Scope

This policy covers the code/trademark separation only. The Apache-2.0 license
governs the code; it grants no general right to use the licensor's product
names or trademarks beyond customary attribution.
