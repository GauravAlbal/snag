# Naming and Attribution Policy

Snag is an independent open-source project maintained by Gaurav Albal.
It is not affiliated with other projects or products using the name Snag.

## Status: not a registered mark

"Snag" is used as the **command name and repository identifier** of the
open-source project maintained by Gaurav Albal (`GauravAlbal/snag`). It is not
a registered trademark, and no exclusive rights to the word "Snag" are claimed.
Do not use `®` or `™` with the name.

The name is shared with several other products in adjacent markets, and a
trademark clearance opinion is required before "Snag" is treated as a durable
commercial brand. The commercial/open-core product line may launch under a
more distinctive house mark; the `snag` CLI and `GauravAlbal/snag` repository
keep their names regardless.

## What this policy allows

- **Accurate attribution.** Refer to the project by name when describing it
  accurately: "Snag", "derived from Snag", "compatible with Snag", "a fork of
  Snag". Identify the source as `GauravAlbal/snag` to avoid confusion with
  other products named Snag.
- **Fork naming.** Forks may describe themselves as derived from or compatible
  with Snag.

## What requires care

- Using "Snag" as the product name of a distribution in a way that implies
  endorsement by or affiliation with `GauravAlbal/snag`.
- Registering or using "Snag" in the AI developer-tooling space in a way that
  could create confusion about the source of the software (several same-market
  products already use the name).

## Scope

The Apache-2.0 license governs the code and grants no general right to use the
project's product names. This policy covers naming and attribution only; it is
not legal advice and does not create or imply any trademark registration.
